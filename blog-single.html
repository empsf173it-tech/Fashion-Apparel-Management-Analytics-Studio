The New Evening Standard: Velvet & Silk

This season, evening wear is redefined by contrasting textures. We explore the juxtaposition of heavy velvet and weightless silk to create a modern silhouette that speaks to quiet luxury.

The Weight of Elegance

Velvet has long been associated with royalty and winter warmth. In the new collection we've sourced a lightweight cotton-blend velvet that drapes beautifully without the stifling heat of traditional weaves â€” the depth of the pile survives, the burden does not.

Fluidity in Motion

To balance the velvet, we introduced silk charmeuse. The way it catches light and moves with the wearer provides a necessary contrast. It isn't only about how it looks, but how it feels against the skin at the eleventh hour of a long evening.

True luxury is found in the spaces between contrast â€” the heavy and the light, the structured and the fluid.
