The Vision

Luxury should be felt, not just seen

VogueMetrics began in a two-room studio in Milan with a stubborn idea: that a garment should be judged by how it behaves after five years, not five minutes.

We source from mills we have visited in person and work with master tailors who have spent careers on a single craft. Our aesthetic bridges classic refinement and contemporary edge â€” restrained on the hanger, quietly assured when worn.

What We Hold To

Three Principles

01

Material Honesty

Every fibre is traceable to its mill. If we cannot name the source, we do not use it.

02

Small Runs

We produce in limited quantities and restock rarely. Scarcity is a consequence, not a tactic.

03

Built to Be Repaired

Complimentary alterations and repairs, for as long as you own the piece.
